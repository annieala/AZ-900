# Microsoft Service Trust Portal

## What Is It?

- A portal that gives access to **content, tools, and resources** about Microsoft's:
  - **Security** practices
  - **Privacy** practices
  - **Compliance** practices
- Explains **how Microsoft implements controls and processes** that protect:
  - Its **cloud services**
  - **Customer data**

## How to Access It

- **URL:** https://servicetrust.microsoft.com/
- Some resources require you to:
  1. **Sign in** as an authenticated user with your **Microsoft cloud services account**
  2. **Review and accept** the Microsoft **non-disclosure agreement (NDA)** for compliance materials

## Main Sections

| Section                  | What It Does                                                                                        |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Service Trust Portal** | Quick link back to the **home page**                                                                |
| **My Library**           | Save (**pin**) documents for quick access; get **notifications** when pinned documents are updated  |
| **All Documents**        | A **single landing place** for all documents; pin documents from here to add them to **My Library** |

## Quick Review

- **Purpose:** learn about Microsoft's security, privacy, and compliance practices
- **Sign-in + NDA** needed for some compliance materials
- **My Library** = your pinned documents + update notifications
- **All Documents** = everything in one place
